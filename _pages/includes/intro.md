# 😊 About me
Hello! 
I am Cuiying Huo, an Assistant Professor at Tianjin University. In January 2025, I obtained my Ph.D. degree from Tianjin University, supervised by Prof. [Di Jin (金弟)](https://cic.tju.edu.cn/faculty/jindi/index.htm) and Prof. [Dongxiao He (何东晓)](https://cic.tju.edu.cn/faculty/hedongxiao/index.htm). Since then, I have been conducting postdoctoral research under the supervision of Prof. [Wenhuan Lu (路文焕)](https://cic.tju.edu.cn/faculty/whlu/index.html), with close academic guidance from Prof. [Jianguo Wei (魏建国)](https://cic.tju.edu.cn/faculty/weijianguo/index.html). From October 2023 to October 2024, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), supervised by Prof. [Gao Cong (丛高)](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/gaocong/). My research interests include graph machine learning, graph neural networks, complex network analysis, and their applications.

霍翠英，天津大学软件学院助理研究员，硕士生导师。长期从事图数据挖掘、可信人工智能、大语言模型等方面的研究。主持国家自然科学基金青年项目，中国博士后科学基金面上项目、国家资助博士后研究人员计划C档项目等。在本领域顶级期刊/会议上发表论文20篇，获得CCF A类会议WWW-2021最佳论文亚军奖（获奖率1‰）、天津大学优秀博士学位论文、天津大学科技创新先进个人等荣誉。长期担任TPAMI、ICML、ICLR、NeurIPS、KDD、WWW、AAAI等CCF A类期刊/会议审稿人，现任中国中文信息学会社会媒体处理专业委员会委员。
